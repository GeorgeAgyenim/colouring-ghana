## Targets (set from the "before" baseline, run B)

Pass rule (method v1): "after" must be less than or equal to "before" on every metric in run B.

- NFR-1.4 time-to-interactive target: at most 22625 ms (median) and 22788 ms (p95) under the run B profile, cold cache.
- Bytes target (whole path, buildings tiles): at most 7,301,105 B (median) and 7,380,168 B (p95).
- Per-tile latency (NFR-1.5 reference): baseline p95 8658 ms (median across repetitions); the NFR-1.5 figure of 200 ms per tile at p95 applies to the archive after the migration.
